Program Pillars

The three pillars of our work are Research & Education, Community Empowerment, and Advocacy & Collaboration. By advancing scientific research, sharing knowledge, and strengthening communities, we create conservation that is not only about protecting forests but also about sustaining the lives of Belitung’s people.

Research & Education

Through ecological research on silvered langurs, we generate knowledge that is shared with local communities, turning science into meaningful conservation action.

Community Empowerment

By fostering sustainable ecotourism and conservation practices in local tourism villages, we make sure that safeguarding nature also strengthens the prosperity of communities.

Advocacy & Collaboration

Working hand in hand with governments, scholars, and communities, we create conservation alliances that safeguard Belitung’s forests for future generations.

Discover White-hair Langur

"An Unforgettable Primate Watching Experience in Tanjung Rusa Tourism Village, Belitung"

Together with Pokdarwis Rusa Putih, we act as facilitators who empower local communities to design their own ecotourism experiences. Lutong Conservation Project provides support, guidance, and conservation expertise so that the tourism village can offer authentic journeys while safeguarding Belitung’s natural heritage. Building on this role, we also facilitate collaboration between villagers, conservationists, and tourism partners, we ensure that the benefits of ecotourism flow directly to the community. This approach not only safeguards biodiversity but also creates new livelihood opportunities, reinforcing the link between conservation and prosperity.
